Coverage and retry limits

Coverage retries are limited to two total research rounds, counting the first round.
The coverage gate retries only uncovered sub-questions and never reruns the planner.
Each source_hint of both dispatches one web worker and one document worker, not a third worker.
Workers share a cumulative maximum of four tool uses per sub-question across search, fetch, document retrieval, and summarization.

When coverage remains incomplete because of round, source, route, or budget limits, pass explicit limitations to the writer.
Insufficient evidence must be disclosed instead of filling gaps with invented facts.
