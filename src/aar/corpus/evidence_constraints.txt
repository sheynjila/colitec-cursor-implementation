Evidence constraints for the Colitech research assistant

Evidence-grounded research assistants must validate every candidate fact against retrieved evidence.
A candidate fact is acceptable only when its exact evidence quote appears in a source retrieved during the same run.
Unknown URLs and absent passages cannot become facts. Missing keys, empty indexes, and unsafe links produce warnings or uncovered work, never fabricated citations.

The writer receives only validated facts and approved references. If coverage is incomplete, the report must say so plainly.
