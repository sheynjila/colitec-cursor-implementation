Routing and budget rules

Model routing scores each sub-question on reasoning depth, context size, tool need, risk, and accuracy need.
Totals of 0 to 4 map to the cheap tier, 5 to 10 map to the standard tier, and 11 to 25 map to the strong tier.
An alias is selected only after enabled status, provider availability, capability, context capacity, required tier, premium policy, and remaining budget are checked.
Prefer the lowest projected cost among eligible aliases. Learned preference may break only same-tier ties.

A zero-budget run must make zero paid model calls. Parallel workers share an atomic budget reservation.
The selected alias must be the alias actually invoked.
